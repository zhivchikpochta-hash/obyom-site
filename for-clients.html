A simple client workflow

From model to approval in three steps.

  1. 1. Choose a modelSend one finished model or portfolio piece. No code and no CAD installation for your client.
  2. 2. Share a private linkYour client opens the model in a browser and checks the shape, scale, and dimensions.
  3. 3. Confirm the versionThe approval belongs to a specific model version, dimensions, and date.
MODELOne exact fileThe approval is tied to a concrete model version and file hash.
SCALEClear dimensionsShow the client the scale, width, height, and depth before production.
STATUSVisible decisionTrack pending, approved, or sent back for changes.
