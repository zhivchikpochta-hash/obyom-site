Use cases

One viewer. Two ways to work with a client.

01

Show your work

Build an interactive portfolio or project page so clients can explore the shape instead of looking at screenshots.

02

Get a model approved

Send a private preview of a specific version, scale, and model so the approval is tied to exactly what goes into work.

For Russian teams, OBYOM is a local foundation for replacing foreign 3D viewer components in client-facing workflows.
